THERAPY™ Run Club × Hotel Sorrento × Nova 100

Same community. New coastline.

This summer, we’re taking the THERAPY™ Run Club to the coast — and bringing the community with us.

Long summer mornings, fresh ocean air, good company and a new Saturday ritual worth waking up for.

We’re kicking things off on Monday 28 December at 8am, with Nova 100’s SuperNovas joining us at Hotel Sorrento for a morning of movement, music, entertainment and summer energy. Then join us — with the SuperNovas and Lululemon — every Saturday at 8am throughout our summer residency.

Start with a coastal run, meet friends for a rooftop smoothie and take time to recover and recharge with THERAPY™ experiences, including hydrogen inhalation, Pulsetto and NeuroSuite. Stay for breakfast, explore Sorrento, settle in for a long lunch or simply enjoy everything the Peninsula has to offer.

And with Nova 100 supporting our summer residency through on-air promotions, social media and a major competition, we’re bringing the THERAPY™ community to an even bigger audience.

Because the best part of wellness isn’t just moving your body. It’s the people you share it with.

One community. One coastline. A whole summer of feeling good.

Good to know

Where is the residency?

At Hotel Sorrento on Victoria’s Mornington Peninsula. Individual experience locations and arrival details will be provided with booking information.

Can I attend if I’m not staying at the hotel?

Locals and visitors are welcome to explore the smoothie offering and community events. Check the booking details for individual experiences and any hotel access conditions.

Are experiences included in my hotel stay?

Please check your accommodation package and the individual experience details. Prices, inclusions and any additional charges will be listed when bookings open.

Are all experiences suitable for everyone?

No. Some services require screening or clinical assessment. Our team can explain what to expect and any relevant considerations before you participate.
