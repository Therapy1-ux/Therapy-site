Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · This policy is pending final legal review.

THERAPY™ (Eltham Wellness Group Pty Ltd, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone) when you enquire, join a waitlist or register interest.
  • Booking, membership and payment information, processed by our booking provider Momence under their own privacy terms.
  • Health information you choose to share with our practitioners, collected with your consent and handled as sensitive information.
  • Basic website analytics (pages visited, device type). We do not sell personal information.

How we use it

  • To provide and schedule services, respond to enquiries and manage memberships.
  • To send service updates and, only with your consent, marketing communications. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet clinical record-keeping and legal obligations.

Storage & security

Information is stored on access-controlled systems. Clinical records are retained as required by Australian health record laws. We take reasonable steps to protect information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Access & contact

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@yourtherapy.au. If unresolved, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
