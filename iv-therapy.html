IV Therapy

Nurse-led. Consultation first, always.

IV therapy at THERAPY™ is delivered by our registered nurses in a private, calm setting. Every appointment begins with a consultation, where your nurse asks about your health, talks you through the options and tells you honestly whether IV therapy is suitable for you.

Book a nurse consultation →

How it works

01

Book a consultation

Choose a time with one of our registered nurses, in person or by phone.

02

Talk it through

Your nurse reviews your health history and what you're looking for. Some therapies also need review by a prescriber.

03

Your appointment

If IV therapy is suitable, your nurse books your appointment. You'll be seated comfortably and monitored throughout.

Good to know

Some IV therapies must be arranged in advance and are not available on the day. Book your consultation at least 7 days before you'd like your appointment.

IV therapy is not suitable for everyone. Your nurse may recommend against it, or suggest you speak with your GP first.

Like any procedure involving a needle, IV therapy carries risks, including bruising, discomfort or infection at the site. Your nurse will explain these before you decide.

Staying at Hotel Sorrento?

IV therapies can be arranged before you arrive. Book a phone consultation with a THERAPY™ nurse at least 7 days before check-in, as some therapies are not available on the day.

Questions

What can I have?

That's what the consultation is for. Your nurse will explain what's available and what each option involves.

How long does it take?

It depends on which treatment you're receiving. Typically allow up to 1 to 1.5 hours.

How much does it cost?

The nurse consultation is $99. Your nurse will confirm the cost of any therapy before you book it.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a consultation directly.
