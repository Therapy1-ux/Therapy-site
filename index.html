Private events · Eltham · 2026

Where every event means something.

An intimate venue reimagining what it means to gather — weddings, celebrations of life, private dinners and corporate days, held in a space unlike anything else in Melbourne's northeast. Exclusive use, always. No shared events.

01 · Clinical

A team, not just a venue.

A genuine clinical team — psychology, myotherapy, registered nurses and functional medicine, all AHPRA-registered, with an in-house GP coming in 2027 — woven into the fabric of your event if you choose.

02 · Experience

Moments they remember.

Breathwork, nervous-system regulation, sound and biohacking — moments your guests will still be talking about a month later. Nothing feels corporate. Nothing feels like filler.

03 · Design

A room with intention.

Every surface, every light, every textile considered. Photographs like an editorial spread. No carpet tiles, no conference tables — a space that holds whatever you bring into it.

The Full Wellness Experience

Three hours, up to thirty guests, private access to the entire wellness centre — a guided rotation through hot & cold therapy, neurosuites, breathwork and meditation. The experience that gives THERAPY™ its name.
