Refer to THERAPY

We welcome referrals from GPs, allied health professionals and practitioners for physiotherapy, myotherapy, psychology, nursing services and structured recovery protocols.

How to refer

Email your referral to hello@yourtherapy.au with the subject “Practitioner referral”, or call us on 0456 582 489.

Please do not include sensitive clinical details in the initial email. Include the client’s name and contact details (with their consent), the service you are referring for, and your practice contact details — our nursing team will follow up securely for any clinical information needed.

EPC & Mental Health Care Plans

We accept Enhanced Primary Care plans for physiotherapy and Medicare-rebated psychology sessions under a Mental Health Care Plan.
